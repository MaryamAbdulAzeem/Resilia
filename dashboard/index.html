AI heat-stress prevention · pilot simulation

Heatstroke gives a warning. Resilia hears it 15 minutes early.

A solar helmet reads heart rate and skin temperature on the worker. An AI agent predicts the danger zone before anyone reaches it, and acts, while a person stays in charge of anything serious.

From a heartbeat to a decision, in four steps

1

Sense

The helmet reads heart rate, skin temperature and movement every few seconds.

2

Predict

The AI adds live site WBGT and forecasts the danger zone up to 15 minutes ahead.

3

Act

Small things happen automatically: a helmet buzz, a water runner, a shade break. Big calls wait for a supervisor.

4

Check

Vitals are watched until they're back to steady, and every step is logged and explainable.
